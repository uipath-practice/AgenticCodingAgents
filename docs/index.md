## What You'll Build

You'll implement a **Benefit Claims** process from the government sector — the procedure citizens
use to request financial or service-based assistance, such as pensions, social assistance, or
unemployment benefits.

Along the way you'll bring together most of the platform. You'll model the end-to-end process as a
BPMN diagram, orchestrate it in **Maestro**, extract data from a claim PDF with **IXP** and an RPA
workflow, build three AI agents that verify residency, verify income, and determine eligibility,
add a human validation step so a case worker makes the final call, and wire up **Integration
Service** to notify the applicant either way.

That's nine steps, each building on the last. By the end you'll have one process that coordinates
robots, agents, and a human — which is what agentic orchestration actually looks like in
production.
